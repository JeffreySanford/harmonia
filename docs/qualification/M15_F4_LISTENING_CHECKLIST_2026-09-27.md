## Suggested listening criteria

For instrumental generators:

- audible non-silent output
- no obvious truncation or corruption
- broadly recognizable requested style or texture
- useful stereo presentation where stereo was requested

For DiffSinger:

- recognizable sung output
- stable note contour
- no dominant corruption or clipping

For ACE-Step:

- recognizable full-song structure
- audible vocal generation
- supplied-language behavior is plausible
- no obvious truncation or corruption

Human taste or artistic preference is not used to invalidate a
technically correct standard qualification result.
